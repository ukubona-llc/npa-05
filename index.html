④ Eigen— · The Structure That Surfaces
The dominant modes
were always there.

When you compress a tensor through a weight matrix, you do not just get scores — you get structure. Districts cluster. Patterns emerge that no analyst spotted in the spreadsheet.

Td×i×t→ Wαᵢ→ vθ→ Eig(Σd)→ κnat
Eigenstructure · NPA Planning Insight
The map doesn't just show where. It shows why the clusters form.
Mode 1 — Structural poverty
Northern and northeastern districts load together on workforce gap, stunting, and facility access. A systemic deficit that runs across all indicators simultaneously.
Mode 2 — Urban complexity
Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono: high facility density but high mental health burden. Resources present, outcomes degraded. A different intervention signature entirely.
Mode 3 — Revenue compliance
Western region districts cluster on CoC performance — they are Uganda's planning model. The eigenmode surfaces them before any analyst writes a report.
Mode 4 — Climate exposure
Eastern flood-prone and northern drought-prone districts form a composite vulnerability mode that predicts health burden, agricultural failure, and fiscal stress simultaneously.
"The eigenstructure is Uganda showing you its skeleton. The map is just the skin."
